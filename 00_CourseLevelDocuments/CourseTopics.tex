\maketitle
\vspace{-2.2em}

\noindent
An introductory Python course for finance masters students:
\textbf{12 lessons of 90 minutes}, taught in English. The course follows the
\emph{python\_psz} (``Programoz\'as alapjai'') materials, adapted to a
quantitative-finance setting. The numbered list mirrors the repository folder
structure one-to-one.

\medskip
\noindent
New material is taught in \textbf{Lessons 1--10}; \textbf{Lesson 11} is practice
plus a mock exam and \textbf{Lesson 12} is the exam itself.

\medskip
\noindent
Four levels are shown: \textbf{lesson} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{topic (source page)}
$\rightarrow$ \textbf{subtopic} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{the exercises set by the
source course}. The exercises are translated from the Hungarian original and are
reproduced \emph{as the source sets them}; they are the raw material to be recast
into finance word problems when the slides are written.

\medskip
\noindent
\textbf{Excluded.} Sorting algorithms (the mirror has four: bubble, insertion, heap,
merge), search algorithms (linear and binary) and \texttt{numpy} are deliberately
not covered.
